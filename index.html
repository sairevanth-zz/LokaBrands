WHO WE ARE: THE NEW STANDARD IN D2C

Elevating the Everyday.

At Loka Brands, we believe that premium quality should not be a geographic privilege. Headquartered in the rapidly growing hub of Warangal, our parent company is engineering an omnichannel consumer ecosystem designed for the modern Indian market.

We do not believe in reinventing the wheel; we believe in perfecting its delivery. Rather than conducting ground-up R&D, our global curation team identifies the most effective, clinically backed products succeeding in Western and East-Asian markets. We then leverage robust domestic manufacturing to bring these transformative solutions to India—bypassing heavy import markups and delivering direct value to our consumers.

From our proprietary digital storefronts to tier-1 marketplaces and premium retail shelves, the Loka Brands umbrella is built for scale, transparency, and trust.

EXECUTIVE STRATEGY & VISION

Letter from the Founder

Sai Revanth Chandupatla — Founder & CEO, Loka Brands

Dear Partners, Investors, and Consumers,

When we established Loka Brands, we recognized a fundamental disparity in the Indian consumer market: Indian consumers were paying exorbitant 60%+ import tariffs and retailer margins for globally proven active skincare and wellness formulations, or settling for mass-market domestic alternatives with diluted ingredient percentages.

Having spent years building high-velocity technology, SaaS, and biotechnology ventures—from Sanjeevani Bio Services in India to AI platforms in the US—I realized that consumer goods needed an agile, software-like execution model. We did not need to spend tens of millions of dollars re-inventing basic chemistry in ground-up labs. The global science already exists in top-tier Western and K-Beauty labs. What India needed was a hyper-efficient distribution and manufacturing engine to democratize access.

Loka Brands was born to bridge that gap. By anchoring our headquarters in Warangal, Telangana, we benefit from exceptional tier-2 operational agility, streamlined cost structures, and direct access to India's burgeoning manufacturing and logistics corridors.

With our Phase 1 launch of Zyrin™, we are proving that luxury K-Beauty Tremella Snow Mushroom actives and bio-peptides can be delivered to every household in India at honest, accessible prices. And Zyrin is just the beginning.

We are building a ₹100 Crore+ house of consumer brands designed for long-term category leadership, radical product transparency, and generational trust. Thank you for joining us on this journey.

Sai Revanth Chandupatla

Founder & CEO, Loka Brands Inc.
